# Glossary

**Arm.** Prepare a track to record. An armed track records when you press Record.

**Automation.** Changes to a setting (volume, pan, an effect control) drawn over time. See
[Automation](automation.md).

**Bar, beat.** Units of musical time. In 4/4 there are four beats in a bar.

**BPM.** Beats per minute: the tempo.

**Buffer size.** How many samples the computer processes at a time. Smaller buffers mean less
latency but more work for the computer.

**Bus.** A track without clips that mixes the signals routed or sent to it. See
[Mixer](mixer.md#buses-and-sends).

**Cents.** Hundredths of a semitone. 100 cents is one semitone.

**Clip.** A piece of audio placed on a track.

**Clipping.** Distortion when a signal goes over the maximum level. Meters turn red.

**Count-in.** Metronome bars played before recording starts.

**dB (decibel).** The unit for levels. 0 dB on a fader means no change; −6 dB is about half the
level.

**Drift.** Slow wandering of pitch within a sung note.

**Dropout (xrun).** A short gap or click in the audio when the computer can't keep up.

**Fade.** A gradual change of volume at the start (fade-in) or end (fade-out) of a clip.

**Fader.** The volume slider of a mixer strip.

**Insert (effect).** An effect placed directly in a track's signal path.

**Key, scale.** The set of notes a song uses, such as G major. Tune's auto-correct moves notes to
the nearest note in the scale.

**Latency.** The delay between a sound going into the computer and coming out. FreeDAW compensates
recordings for it.

**Loop region.** The part of the timeline that repeats while **Loop** is on, and where punch
recording happens.

**Master.** The final mix, which goes to your speakers.

**Monitoring.** Hearing an input through FreeDAW while recording or rehearsing.

**Mute, solo.** Mute silences a track. Solo plays only the soloed tracks.

**Pan.** A track's left/right position in the stereo image.

**Playhead.** The vertical line showing the current playback position.

**Post-fader, pre-fader.** Whether a send follows the track's fader (post) or not (pre).

**Punch in/out.** Recording only part of the timeline, replacing a section of a take.

**Quantize.** Moving notes toward a rhythmic grid.

**Sample rate.** How many times per second the audio is measured, for example 48 kHz.

**Semitone.** The smallest step between notes on a piano: from C to C♯.

**Send.** A copy of a track's signal sent to a bus, often to share an effect.

**Snap.** Making moves and edits align to the grid.

**Take.** One recording pass.

**Track.** A row in the timeline holding clips, with its own volume, pan and effects.

**Vibrato.** A regular, fast wobble in pitch, used expressively by singers and players.
