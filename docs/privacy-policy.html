FreeDAW Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

FreeDAW is a free audio workstation for recording, editing and mixing music, available as a web edition and a desktop edition (Windows, macOS and Linux). This policy is short because the answer is short: FreeDAW does not collect, transmit or share personal data with anyone, including the developer.

What data the app uses

Your projects, recordings, imported audio files and settings are saved only on your own device:

  • Web edition: in your browser's private storage for this site (the origin private file system for projects and recordings, local storage for settings such as the audio device and the latency calibration).
  • Desktop edition: in the app's data folder on your computer.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it, for example by saving a .freedaw project file to a location you pick.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login or registration.
  • No server or backend of its own: your audio is processed on your device.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising or third-party crash reporting.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Microphone and audio input

FreeDAW asks for access to your microphone or audio interface only when you select an input device to record or to calibrate latency. The audio it captures is written to your project on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Update check

When you open the About dialog, FreeDAW asks GitHub for the latest published release so it can tell you whether an update is available. Nothing about you or your projects is included in that request. FreeDAW makes no other network requests of its own; links in the app (such as the source code or sponsorship pages) open in your browser only when you click them.

Web hosting

The web edition is hosted on GitHub Pages, at campos20.github.io/freedaw. As with any website, GitHub receives standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page or when the app checks for updates. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement, and none of it is shared with the developer.

Data deletion

Since everything is saved only on your device, deleting a project removes it permanently; there is no server-side copy. In the web edition, clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all projects and settings. In the desktop edition, uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder does the same.

Children

FreeDAW is a general-purpose music tool. Because it does not collect or transmit personal data from any user, adult or child, no children's data is collected, used or shared.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with the date at the top updated.
